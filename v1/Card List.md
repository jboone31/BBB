## Go Piss Girl
At least one member of the target team must enter a publicly accessible restroom that is not located inside any bar.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team, toast message + confirmation by target team. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.

## Crop Dusting
**Special Rule:** Does not target a team.
**Requirements:** Must be played physically inside any bar that is not the finish bar.
Once played in a bar, no other team may enter that bar for 15 minutes.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a bar, toast message alert to all non-casting teams. Prevent target bar from being claimed for 15 minutes.

## Moneybags
The target team must find a product with a visible price tag of more than $150 inside any retail store. A photograph of the price tag is required for verification.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team, toast message + confirmation by target team. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.

## Use It or Lose It
The target team must immediately play every card currently in their hand. If a card has a playing requirement that the team cannot meet at that moment, that card is discarded with no effect.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team, prompts them to play all cards before claiming a bar. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.

## Wired

## Everyone's a Critic
The target team must take a picture on the premises of a location with at least 4.5 stars on Google. If they fail, they may not claim a bar for 15 minutes. The cursed team may not use the internet for research until making an attempt.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team, toast message + confirmation by target team. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.

## Fairest of Them All
**Requirements:** May only be played immediately after your team is targeted by a challenge card.
Force the casting team to also complete the challenge card's requirements (both teams complete it).

***App Mechanics:*** When team is targeted by a card, if this card is in their hand, they are prompted to play it as part of the same toast message as the original challenge. If they do, the casting team is alerted they have been mirrored and shown the same original challenge toast message. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.
## Pioneer
The next bar claimed by the target team must be an unclaimed bar that is not the finish bar.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team, toast message. Target team is not allowed to claim the finish bar or a bar that has already been claimed by another team for the next claim only. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.
## Cancel Culture
**Special Rule:** Targets a bar that is not within 0.25 miles of the finish bar, and this bar must be publicly announced on play.
Beginning 15 minutes after the target bar is announced by the casting team, no bar within 0.25 miles of the target bar may be claimed for the next 15 minutes.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a bar. Toast message to all teams stating that in fifteen minutes, no bar within 0.25 miles may be claimed. Another toast message to all teams when no-claim period starts. Uses coordinates of bar to disallow claiming of affected bars for fifteen minutes.

## Scenic Route
The target team's next claimed bar must be farther from the finish bar than the last bar their team claimed.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team, toast message. Uses coordinates of bar to disallow target team from claiming a bar closer to the finish than the last bar they claimed for next claim only. Informational toast message to non-target/casting teams.

## Insurance
**Special Rule:** Targets the casting team only.
The points your team receives from the next bar you claim cannot be reduced by later-claiming teams. Other teams receive points for claiming this bar as if they were sharing normally.

***App Mechanics:*** Ensures points are not reduced for casting team only for the next claimed bar. Toast message to all non-casting teams.

## Happy Hour
**Special Rule:** Targets any bar that is not the finish bar.
Any team that drinks at the target bar in the next hour gets +5 points for the first drink only, even if they have already claimed it.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a bar, toast message to all teams. Adds five immutable points to any team that claims the bar alongside normal point-sharing, and allows teams that have already claimed the bar to claim it again for only those five points (claim is unaffected).

## Party Crasher
**Special Rule:** Targets any bar that is not the finish bar and that fewer than four teams have claimed.
Teams claiming this bar score as if sharing with one extra (phantom) team. If four teams claim it, the phantom share disappears and all teams score the normal 3 each.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a bar that fewer than four teams have claimed, reduces points for teams perpetually as if another phantom team was sharing. Toast message to all teams.

## Patient Investor
**Special Rule:** Targets the casting team only.
After your team claims four more bars, gain +6 points.

***App Mechanics:*** Toast message to all non-casting teams. Tracks when casting team claims four more bars, and adds six immutable points afterward.

## Power Hour
**Special Rule:** No target; persistent effect.
For the next twenty minutes, whenever any team claims a bar, that team draws two cards and keeps both.

***App Mechanics:*** Toast message to all teams. Ensures cards are not discarded whenever any team claims a bar for twenty minutes.

## Voted Off the Island
**Requirements:** All four teams have claimed a non-starting bar.
The target team un-claims a bar that all four teams have claimed. They may choose to re-claim the bar.

***App Mechanics:*** Targets a team AND a bar that all four teams have claimed. Toast message to target team that this bar has been un-claimed for them, and allows team to re-claim bar. Informational toast message to other teams.
